# No-LLM/genAI policy

During the recent years, there has been a rise in popularity for tools
popularly known as "generative AI" or "large language models"
(LLMs, for short). These tools are fed with incredibly vast amounts of
human-generated content, often copyrighted, without explicit permission
from its originals authors. On the other hand, the hardware required to
train the models used by these tools requires large amounts of energy,
contributing to climate change. Moreover, many of these tools are proprietary.

We, the Speed Dreams community, highly value human contributions.
Over the course of more than 30 years, RARS, TORCS and then Speed Dreams
have accumulated a big knowledge base made by hundreds of human authors,
each one of them with their own backgrounds and skills.

And we do not want this to change.

Free software is all about *humans* collaborating for the commons.
Aside from the possible legal implications behind LLM-generated contributions,
and regardless their perceived convenience or usefulness, shifting the
work behind an otherwise human contribution to a LLM defeats the purpose
of free software.

Therefore, this project **strictly rejects genAI/LLM-generated contributions**.
In other words, the use of tools such as Claude, GitHub Copilot, Midjourney,
ChatGPT or any other similar tool is not allowed, and any contributions shall
be dismissed. This includes, but is not limited to, code, art and/or translation
contributions.

We want humans collaborating with other humans into making a great, free
(as in freedom) video game. We want a friendly, passionate community.
We believe none of this is possible with tools that aim to replace human
thinking.
